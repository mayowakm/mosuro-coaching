Clients & data

Clients, data & share

Manage every client from here, hand each one their own workout logger, and pull their logged sessions back in.

Your clients

Switch between clients any time from the Client selector at the top. Each client keeps their own program, phases, logs, and equipment.

How the client logger works. There’s no shared server, so each client uses their own copy of the logger file. Give them their setup code once (below) — it loads their name, email, and current phase workouts into the logger. They log in your absence, tap Share with coach, and send you the result; paste it into Import from client and it routes to the right client automatically. You can also log in-person sessions yourself in the Workouts tab.

Client logger setup code

Generates a code for the current client that loads their identity + current phase workouts into the logger. Send it with their logger file.

Import from client

Paste what a client sent from their logger (the whole message is fine). It loads into the matching client — by email, then name.

Present to client

Opens a clean, full-page view of every tab — ideal for screen-sharing or saving as PDF (choose "Save as PDF" in the print dialog).

Back up & transfer

Export a backup of all clients, or restore from one. Import replaces all current data.

Current client details

Headline details that drive this client’s dashboard. Email is used to route their logged sessions back to them.

Reset

Clear all clients and data and start fresh. This cannot be undone — export a backup first.
